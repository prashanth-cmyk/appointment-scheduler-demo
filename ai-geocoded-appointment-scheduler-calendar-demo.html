    How to use Step-by-step operator guide

    1. 1 Enter your NextBillion API key in the top bar (stored in this browser session only).
    2. 2 Click Start desk — no city or service center is pre-selected. This unlocks the booking calendar and a US overview map.
    3. 3 Fill Incoming appointment: customer name, optional phone, service type, then search a service address (Autocomplete / Geocode). Only then does the desk pick the nearest service center and nearest technician on that center’s roster.
    4. 4 Under Choose a day, click an available calendar date (teal-bordered days). Days outside the booking window or Sundays are disabled.
    5. 5 Wait briefly while the desk scores feasible hourly slots for the assigned technician with Distance Matrix and refreshes their route on the map.
    6. 6 In Available times, pick an NB-ranked slot (Best / Recommended / Available). Review the confirm panel, then click Book.
    7. 7 Check the Assigned service center & technician board and Route map. Book another customer or day as needed.
    8. 8 Use Reset day to restore that day’s seed schedules and re-run road optimization.
    Tip: Calendar days unlock after Start desk. Service center + technician appear only after you locate an address. Slots appear after you select a day and enter a customer name.

    App logic What this desk is simulating

    This is a field-service appointment desk: an operator captures the customer, picks a calendar day, and the system recommends slots that fit a technician’s day with the least extra driving. Each visit takes 30 minutes of on-site service. Booking slots are offered on the hour (09:00–10:00 … 17:00–18:00). Technicians work 9:00 AM–6:00 PM.

    Unlike the map-first demo (fixed “Day 1”), this UI keeps separate schedules per calendar day. Each of the 15 US cities has three technicians with depots and seed appointments in that metro. The operator searches a real address (Autocomplete / Geocode) with no city pre-selected; the desk then picks the nearest service center and the nearest technician on that center’s roster, and ranks slots against that tech’s day.

    Distance Matrix API Road travel times between points

    Endpoint: GET /distancematrix/json (driving mode). Answers: “How long does it take to drive from A to B on the road network?”

    Where it is used:

    • Smart slot recommendation — Checks whether a technician can leave the previous job (or depot), travel to the new customer, arrive by the slot start, finish 30 minutes of service, and still reach the next appointment.
    • Ranking — Estimates extra travel from inserting the stop (insertion cost). Lower extra travel ranks higher.
    • Waiting time on the technician board — waiting = scheduled start − (previous finish + travel).
    When you see it run: after you select a day (with a customer name entered), or when refreshing waiting times after book/reset. If Distance Matrix is unavailable, the demo falls back to an approximate travel estimate so the UI still works.

    Route Optimization API Assign & optimize the technician’s day

    Endpoints: POST /optimization/v2, then GET /optimization/v2/result?id=… until routes are ready.

    Builds an optimized day plan using jobs, time windows, service duration, vehicle shift hours, and a travel-time objective. Requests road geometry (with_geometry: true) so routes follow actual road segments on the map.

    Where it is used:

    • Day select — Every technician with appointments that day is optimized so seed (and prior) routes show as road polylines, not straight lines.
    • After booking — The new appointment is added to the chosen technician; Route Optimization re-runs for that technician only.
    • Reset day — Seed schedules are restored and all technicians are optimized again.

    Key settings: 30-minute service, preferred hour as time_windows, shift time_window 9:00–18:00, objective.travel_cost: "duration".

    When you see it run: status shows “Optimizing all technician routes…” on day select / reset, and “Booking & re-optimizing…” on book.

    Maps SDK Visualization only

    The NextBillion Web Maps SDK renders the city map, depot markers, numbered stops, and optimized route lines for the selected calendar day.

    Important: Maps are for display. Slot ranking and route planning come from Distance Matrix + Route Optimization (plus local scheduling rules), not from the map itself.

    Step-by-step process (system view)

    Step What happens API / logic
    1. Start desk Set booking window, unlock calendar, load locations & map. App data + Maps SDK
    2. Capture customer Name, location, service type for the incoming appointment. App UI
    3. Select day Load that day’s technicians and seed appointments. App multi-day state
    4. Seed / day optimize Road geometry for every tech with jobs on that day. Route Optimization
    5. Waiting times Estimate arrival and idle time before each visit. Distance Matrix + app formula
    6. Recommend slots Test each hour × each technician; show top ranked slots. Distance Matrix + app scoring
    7. Book Assign appointment to the chosen technician and hour. App logic
    8. Re-optimize Rebuild that technician’s day; update map & board. Route Optimization + Maps + DM

    Recommendation scoring (simple view)

    For a candidate technician and time slot, the demo:

    • Rejects the slot if it is already booked or outside the 9:00–6:00 shift.
    • Uses Distance Matrix for travel from the previous stop (or depot) to the customer, and from the customer to the next stop.
    • Checks arrival before the slot start and that the next appointment is still reachable after 30 minutes of service.
    • Scores mainly by extra travel time (insertion cost): travel(previous → new) + travel(new → next) − travel(previous → next). Lower extra travel ranks higher.
    • Applies a small preference for less busy technicians and for filling natural gaps in the day.

    The best technician for each hour is kept, then the top options are shown as NB recommended slots — not every empty hour on the calendar.

    Waiting time on technician cards: waiting = max(0, appointment start − (previous finish + travel to this stop)).
